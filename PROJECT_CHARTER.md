# Project charter

**Project.** ChudiCorp: From Digital Product Development to Evidence-Led Thought Leadership.

**Owner and accountable decision-maker.** Chudi Nwachukwu.

**Period.**
- Website development: project created May 2026; intensive implementation and assurance in October 2026.
- Educational programme: prepared and scheduled for November 2026.
- Portfolio assurance (Stages A–C): November 2026.
- Performance evaluation: to follow.

**Background.** Developing ChudiCorp.com gave practical experience in:
- turning business requirements into working functions;
- managing V1 scope;
- reviewing test evidence;
- dealing with operational risks;
- deciding when something is ready.

That knowledge was spread across briefs, decisions, test reports, security reviews and release records.

**Problem statement.** Turn documented development experience into accessible, accurate and useful educational material, without overstating achievements, misrepresenting technical results or exposing confidential information.

**Objective.** Deliver a 15-part LinkedIn knowledge-sharing programme with 15 supporting graphics. The programme should demonstrate practical Business Analysis, Product and Project Management, Data Analytics, Systems Integration and responsible AI principles. Where appropriate, each topic is linked to documented ChudiCorp milestones and known limitations.

**Scope (in)**
- Topic selection and five-workstream structure.
- 15 posts and 15 graphics.
- Editorial and disclosure review.
- Manual scheduling.
- The later Stage A evidence matrix and Stage B visual review.
- Stage C GitHub and Behance documentation.
- A performance and retrospective framework.

**Scope (out)**
- Changes to the ChudiCorp website.
- Publishing source code, customer or payment identifiers, credentials, admin screens or security mechanisms.
- Claims of client delivery, earned revenue, indexing, traffic or engagement that are not supported by evidence.

**Roles**

| Party | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chudi Nwachukwu | Objectives, topics, priorities, decisions, approvals, owner checks, scheduling |
| ChatGPT | Research, analysis, drafting, visual preparation, critical review (owner-confirmed) |
| Lovable | Website implementation and technical records; the later Stage A–C portfolio reviews |

**Constraints**
- Public-safety rules apply.
- Evidence limitations accepted in Stages A and B carry forward unchanged.
- The approved captions and graphics are never edited.

**Risks and controls**

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Overclaiming | Two-dimension evidence labels and post-level limitations |
| Oversharing | Public-safety rule and quality gate |
| Attribution blur | Separate statements of each party's role |
| Chronology distortion | The fixed sequence shown at the top of this document |

**Success criteria**
1. 15 posts and graphics approved and scheduled. **Met.**
2. Every public claim traceable and labelled. **Met in Stage A and Stage B.**
3. GitHub and Behance materials public-safe and distinct from each other. **Stage C drafts complete.**
4. Actual performance evidence incorporated once verified. **Pending.**
